2. CANDIDATE SEMANTIC DEFINITIONS
======================================================================

A. CREDENTIAL_CUSTODY_INHERITANCE

Question:

Did credential-bearing material or a credential-bearing private object
under custody of one participant/generation become available to a
successor without a new canonically authorized custody assignment?

Possible objects may include only objects whose credential-bearing
semantics are independently established.

DO NOT infer credential-bearing semantics from the word "credential".

PUBLIC MANIFEST DATA MUST NOT automatically become credential material.

------------------------------------------------------------

B. CREDENTIAL_AUTHORITY_INHERITANCE

Question:

Can a successor exercise credential-derived authority belonging to a
prior participant/generation without receiving a new valid authority
binding?

Secret copying is NOT required for this condition.

Possession is NOT sufficient for this condition.

The question is effective authority.

------------------------------------------------------------

C. CREDENTIAL_BINDING_INHERITANCE

Question:

Does a credential reference, descriptor, or authority-bearing binding
remain valid across a transition that renews subject, generation, or
process identity where the relevant binding should have been renewed
or requalified?

This dimension concerns binding continuity.

It MUST NOT silently collapse into custody or effective authority.
