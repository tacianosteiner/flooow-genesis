## V2 product thesis

Room V2 is not a collection of four dashboards.

It is one governed economic reality observed through executive lenses.

The same Economic Truth must remain upstream for CEO, CFO, COO and CRO.
No executive lens may independently redefine truth.

### CEO

Primary question:

> Is the business healthy, what changed, where is value at risk, and what requires my decision?

Focus:

- economic state
- material risks
- material opportunities
- unresolved exceptions
- decision pressure
- organizational readiness
- confidence / evidence posture

### CFO

Primary question:

> Do the numbers close, where is economic value leaking, and how trustworthy is the financial state?

Focus:

- real contribution and margin
- economic leakage
- reconciliation
- divergence
- cost structure
- financial exposure
- evidence completeness
- confidence

### COO

Primary question:

> What is failing operationally, what is blocked, and what economic impact is being created?

Focus:

- exceptions
- bottlenecks
- operational failures
- SLA / process state
- reconciliation pressure
- recovery eligibility
- economic consequence

### CRO

Primary question:

> Where is observed revenue or margin under pressure, and where does governed evidence justify commercial attention?

Focus:

- observed revenue
- contribution
- product/channel/customer pressure
- commercial signals
- opportunity evidence
- margin-aware growth

Forecasts and opportunities must not be invented from missing evidence.

## Shared institutional spine

All executive lenses are projections over the same governed institutional reality:

1. Economic Truth
2. Reconciliation
3. Decisions
4. System Health / Governance

These are shared foundations, not separate products.

## Product principles

### 1. Decision-first

Every important number should help answer:

- What changed?
- Why does it matter?
- How certain are we?
- What requires attention?
- What decision is eligible?
- What remains blocked?

### 2. Truth-first

Models, heuristics, opportunity intelligence and future AI assistance may interpret evidence.

They may not redefine canonical truth.

### 3. Exception-first

Executives should not need to inspect every transaction.

The Room should surface material deviations, pressure, unresolved state and decision relevance.

### 4. One organism, multiple lenses

CEO, CFO, COO and CRO must feel like different views of the same economic organism.

They must not become disconnected dashboards.

### 5. Evidence visible when needed

Executive simplicity must never erase provenance.

Users must be able to move from signal to evidence and institutional source.

### 6. Fail closed

Missing authority, missing evidence, incomplete reconciliation or insufficient readiness must never be visually presented as approval.

### 7. Preview does not grant authority

Room V2 remains a read / interpret / decide-support surface.

At bootstrap:

`ROOM_OPERATIONAL = NO`

No UI treatment may imply execution authority.

## V2 visual direction

The target is institutional decision infrastructure rather than a generic SaaS dashboard.

Desired characteristics:

- executive information hierarchy
- restrained density
- strong typographic hierarchy
- high information-to-decoration ratio
- sovereign / institutional visual language
- explicit economic state
- materiality before volume
- exception and decision posture above raw tables
- drill-down only after executive synthesis
- provenance and governance available without visual clutter

Avoid:

- playful fintech styling
- arbitrary KPI-card grids
- excessive gradients
- generic AI assistant appearance
- unexplained scores
- decorative charts without decision value
- green states that imply authority when authority is absent

## V2 first implementation boundary

The first V2 implementation may improve:

- information architecture
