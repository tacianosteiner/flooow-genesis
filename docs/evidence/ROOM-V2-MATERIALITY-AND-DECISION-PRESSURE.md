# FLOOOW Room V2 - Materiality & Decision Pressure

Date: 2026-10-12

Branch: feature/room-v2-executive-product-evolution

Baseline: a079ba5670329b2755d612da5b91c7d676a1ba29

## Intent

Surface what requires executive attention without inventing monetary materiality.

## Implemented

- Added a shared Decision Pressure board.
- Pressure is derived only from existing observed truth/governance state.
- Unresolved truth is BLOCKING.
- Economic incompleteness is ATTENTION.
- Open reconciliation is ATTENTION when present.
- Systemic divergence is ATTENTION when present.
- Execution authority remains BLOCKED while ROOM_OPERATIONAL=NO.
- Monetary materiality is explicitly UNQUANTIFIED when the current projection does not support a defensible amount.
- No priority score was invented.
- No monetary leakage amount was invented.
- No forecast or opportunity data was introduced.

## Governance boundary

Decision pressure is not the same as quantified economic materiality.

A high-pressure item can be operationally or epistemically blocking while its economic amount remains unknown.

The UI must preserve that distinction.
