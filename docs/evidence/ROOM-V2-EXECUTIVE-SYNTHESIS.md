# FLOOOW Room V2 - Executive Synthesis

Date: 2026-10-12

Branch: feature/room-v2-executive-product-evolution

Baseline: 84ec7574f3c4740ab52c495b4735456218245004

## Intent

Move the Room from metric presentation toward executive interpretation without inventing truth, forecasts, authority or unsupported opportunity.

## Implemented

- Added an Executive Synthesis surface to CEO, CFO, COO and CRO.
- Each synthesis answers current state, why it matters, what deserves attention and the authority boundary.
- CEO prioritizes unresolved truth, open reconciliation and systemic divergence.
- CFO makes incompleteness and reconciliation constrain financial confidence.
- COO treats unresolved truth and systemic divergence as operational exceptions.
- CRO remains explicitly limited to observed revenue and margin.
- All synthesis is derived only from existing Room state already loaded into the UI.
- No new score, forecast, model or backend field was introduced.

## Governance boundary

- ROOM_OPERATIONAL remains NO.
- Execution authority remains blocked.
- Detection does not grant recovery.
- Missing evidence is not transformed into zero or estimate.
- Commercial opportunity is not inferred without supporting evidence.
- Package 0090 unchanged.
- main unchanged.
