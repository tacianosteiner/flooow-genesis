## Separate authorization and noninterference

After Room V1 operational proof and offline qualification, request bounded shadow authority with approved read-only mirror, rights, minimization, artifact identities, operator, expiry and resource budget. No shadow deployment is authorized now. Room canonical flow continues independently; shadow may propose relationships, not alter identity, execute tools, write production or allocate capital.

Outputs carry input evidence refs, taxonomy/model revision, context, scores/calibration, abstention reason, timing, cost and correction lineage. Model proposes -> human adjudicates -> evidence recorded -> versioned dataset improves. Independent adjudication compares known mappings, operational outcomes and subsequent corrections; suggestions do not certify their own labels.

Monitor precision, recall where observable, false matches by risky relation, coverage/abstention, stability, drift, latency, cost, missing-source and privacy violations. A shadow sample has selection bias; document cohort and uncertainty. Pre-register exposure, minimum evidence and stop/rollback rules. Missing numerical acceptance/stop thresholds blocks activation, not an assumed default. Disable shadow on authority violation, rights failure or dangerous errors above the approved budget; preserve evidence without impacting Room.
