## Cohort A — commerce/shopper deep review

### Similarweb: demand and consideration before commercial confirmation

[E01–E06](global-signal/SOURCE-EVIDENCE-REGISTER.md) distinguish cross-retailer behavior, Amazon sales signals and catalog/shelf fields. Granularity spans SKU, keyword, category, brand and retailer; the offer includes views, traffic sources, cross-shopping and basket/loyalty analysis. The scale claims requested in the mission are present publicly and labeled SELF_REPORTED_VENDOR_CLAIM. API, feeds, AI Studio and MCP are documented; they require entitlement and cannot remove source uncertainty.

The useful problem is discovering consideration that transactional data alone misses. Inferred mechanism: observed behavior plus vendor aggregation/modeling, not universal verified orders. Benchmark against matched, rights-cleared retailer records and a public search/shelf baseline. The superiority hypothesis is earlier category discovery after reporting latency, without greater false positives.

Cadence cannot be collapsed into “live”: retail metric documentation specifies weekly/weekly-monthly delivery with nine-day lag, while cross-retail excludes app/offline traffic. Generic API history does not prove retail-SKU history. Country/retailer totals do not prove equivalent field coverage everywhere. Review endpoint-specific estimator definitions, sample stability, suppressed cohorts, history, match errors, quota and price before an empirical comparison. Disposition BENCHMARK; no foundational provider selection.

### Stackline: Atlas outside-in, Beacon inside-out

[E07–E10](global-signal/SOURCE-EVIDENCE-REGISTER.md) separate market/category intelligence in Atlas from a brand's operations in Beacon. Atlas advertises sales/share, search, traffic and advertising comparisons; claimed scale and accuracy remain self-reported. Daily product measurement and weekly share reporting are separate statements, not a measured latency guarantee.

Beacon advertises sales/margins, conversion, advertising, reviews/content and SKU forecasting. A 52-week horizon is a forecast horizon, not historical retention or accuracy proof. Brand-integrated records may have first-party inputs; their reconciliation, transformation and authorization must still be demonstrated. “1P/3P sales” retailer models must not be confused with evidence provenance.

Atlas Segment API/Snowflake is an announced delivery path. A search result for beacon.com API belongs to another business and was excluded. Beacon API/export scope therefore remains UNKNOWN. Compare category estimates and authorized brand actuals separately; ask whether forecasts beat seasonal/naive baselines out of time and whether earlier signals improve contribution rather than only sales. Disposition BENCHMARK for both; automation/activation claims confer no FLOOOW execution authority.

## Cohort B — separate attention from demand

[E17–E30](global-signal/SOURCE-EVIDENCE-REGISTER.md) preserve six distinct signal kinds: ATTENTION, ADVERTISING_ACTIVITY, SEARCH_INTENT, CREATOR_VELOCITY, PRODUCT_MENTION_VELOCITY and COMMERCIAL_CONVERSION. Mention velocity requires identity adjudication and repeated comparable observations; an isolated popular post is insufficient.

TikTok Creative Center is a curated creative/trend resource. Region, industry and time filters define the denominator; Top Products concerns ad popularity. TikTok One is a contractual creative workflow, not the same public dataset. Top Ads has winner-selection/survivorship bias, and public documentation does not verify export/API/history completeness.

Meta's public interface and API are different. The official API description currently includes global political/issue ads for seven years and any-type ads delivered to UK/EU for one year. It does not expose all ordinary commercial ads globally. Account/identity/developer authorization applies; demographic reach is estimated and political spend/impressions are ranges. A Brazilian commercial UI observation must not be promoted into global API archival coverage.

Google Ads Transparency is advertiser/creative activity, not conversion. Google Trends is sampled normalized search interest with noise, suppression and window-specific timezone; its documented API is limited alpha. Merchant API manages authorized account/product inputs and does not grant arbitrary competitor orders. Pinterest's regional search/save/shopping features and YouTube's region/category discovery provide alternative planning/creator contexts, with sampling and historical gaps. Compare attention against independent purchase evidence before drawing demand conclusions.
