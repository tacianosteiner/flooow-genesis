## Rubric

STRONG = explicit documentation of a capability central to the named product, without implying measured quality. PARTIAL = constrained/mixed/authorized/subset coverage or an indirect proxy. WEAK = weak proxy or inadequate support for the required role. UNKNOWN = insufficient documentation for the precise use; never absence. NOT_APPLICABLE = not part of the defined source's purpose (not a claim it is impossible).

Vendor STRONG is a documented marketing capability, not verified accuracy, completeness or access. All underlying data capabilities are REPRODUCED=NO. REPRODUCIBILITY rates currently available evidence: WEAK means public descriptions exist but no dataset replay/error reproduction; UNKNOWN means source-specific capture/rights still unresolved. PROVENANCE means traceable official documentation plus methodological limitations, not trusted transactions. FRESHNESS evaluates explicit cadence disclosure, not observed latency; HISTORICAL evaluates documented retention scope, not verified backfill. No numeric scores or provider selection.

Catalog/evidence links identify the basis for each row. China hypotheses are PARTIAL at most where a marketplace role is supported; listed SALES/PURCHASE hypotheses remain UNKNOWN without observed scoped transactions. Search demand columns describe search proxies, not confirmed demand.

## Comparison decision

Similarweb and Atlas target outside-in market/consideration intelligence; Beacon targets authorized brand operations; NIQ distinguishes measurement inputs and projections; Profitero/DataWeave specialize in shelf observations. TikTok/Meta/Google/Pinterest/YouTube expose different attention/search/ad contexts, not equivalent commercial outcomes. MikMak/FastMoss are bounded specialist challengers; access and accuracy remain unqualified. Official trade statistics corroborate trade scope, not SKU demand.

The matrix intentionally retains UNKNOWN across unsupported dimensions. Provider ranking, cost advantage, lead time, forecast superiority and causal attribution cannot be determined by this matrix. Next comparison requires a rights-cleared corpus, source-specific denominator and independent reproduction with adversarial negatives.
