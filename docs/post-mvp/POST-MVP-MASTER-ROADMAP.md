## Sequence and prerequisite gates

```text
ROOM V1 OPERATIONAL
  -> OUTCOME / LEARNING BASELINE
  -> POST-MVP-0001 PRODUCT INTELLIGENCE BENCHMARK
  -> POST-MVP-0002 EVIDENCE RETRIEVAL ENGINE
  -> POST-MVP-0003 OPPORTUNITY ENGINE V1
  -> REDMOTO REAL LAB
  -> POST-MVP-0004 PREMIUM BEVERAGE INTELLIGENCE LAB
  -> POST-MVP-0005 FORESIGHT ENGINE
  -> POST-MVP-0006 CREATOR / DISTRIBUTION INTELLIGENCE
  -> POST-MVP-0007 CAPITAL OPPORTUNITY ENGINE
  -> OPPORTUNITY FOUNDRY / GOVERNED CAPITAL LAYER
```

This orders dependencies, not delivery dates or current completion claims. Room V1 remains operational and receives real data once operational proof exists; experimental availability must never become a Room availability dependency.

| Program | Required evidence before advancing |
|---|---|
| Room baseline | Immutable operational milestone, governed authority and reconciled real outcomes |
| 0001 | Rights-cleared adjudicated dataset, deterministic baseline, protected evaluation and adversarial results |
| 0002 | Stable benchmark architecture; independently sourced support and contradictions with provenance |
| 0003 | Usable product/retrieval capabilities and populated economic truth; candidate uncertainty and kill rules |
| Redmoto | Real adjudicated inputs and risk-adjusted comparison including DO_NOT_INVEST; R$1M is a research scenario, not allocation authority |
| 0004 | Prior foundations usable; physical retail, rights, control design and contribution reconciliation |
| 0005 | Adequate time-series truth; calibration and forecast error history; FORECAST never becomes ACTUAL |
| 0006 | Qualified reach, overlap, economics and observed conversion; followers alone do not establish distribution |
| 0007 / Foundry | Reliable opportunity evidence, calibrated uncertainty, real outcome history and explicit capital gates |

Every new capability follows DISCOVER -> DATA CONTRACT -> BASELINE -> BENCHMARK -> ADVERSARIAL -> SHADOW -> PILOT -> PROMOTION GATE -> ROOM UPGRADE. No large rewrite of Room V1. Capital is earned by evidence; every future tranche needs CAPITAL_REQUIRED, MAXIMUM_LOSS, EVIDENCE_REQUIRED, WHAT_MUST_BE_TRUE, KILL_RULE and NEXT_BEST_INFORMATION. No public investment marketplace is authorized.
