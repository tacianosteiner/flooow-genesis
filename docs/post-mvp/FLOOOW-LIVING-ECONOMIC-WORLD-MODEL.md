## 1. Purpose

The Living Economic World Model (LEWM) is the governed world-context
capability of FLOOOW.

Its purpose is to continuously observe relevant economic, commercial,
financial, technological, geopolitical, regulatory and behavioral signals
and maintain explicit competing interpretations of the external world.

LEWM is not a source of automatic truth.

It exists to improve context, scenario reasoning, regime awareness,
forecasting and decision quality while preserving the authority boundaries
of Economic Truth.

Core chain:

Signal
→ Regime Evidence
→ Competing Hypotheses
→ Governed Belief
→ Scenario Context
→ Decision Context

Never:

Signal
→ automatic truth

or:

Forecast
→ authority

or:

LEWM belief
→ canonical Economic Truth

## 2. Constitutional boundary

LEWM context may influence a governed decision process.

## 4. Required contracts

P8 must eventually define explicit contracts for:

### Economic Regime

A bounded interpretation of the current external economic environment.

Required properties should include:

- identity;
- temporal validity;
- supporting evidence;
- contradictory evidence;
- provenance;
- confidence;
- uncertainty;
- supersession lineage.

### World Signal

An externally observed or derived signal.

A World Signal is evidence input, not truth.

### Competing Hypothesis

Multiple explanations may coexist.

The system must preserve disagreement rather than prematurely collapse it.

### Temporal State

Every world-state assertion requires temporal scope.

Current, stale, historical and projected states must remain distinguishable.

### Scenario Context

A scenario is a governed conditional model of a possible future.

Scenario != forecast truth.
Scenario != decision.
Scenario != authority.

### Forecast Context

Forecasts require:

- model identity;
- version;
- assumptions;
- horizon;
- provenance;
- uncertainty;
- calibration evidence where available;
- actual-outcome reconciliation.

## 11. Strategic horizon

The purpose of LEWM is not to predict the future perfectly.

Its purpose is to let FLOOOW reason about a changing world without confusing
uncertainty with truth.

The long-term capability is a living, evidence-bound world context that
helps an organization understand:

- what may be changing;
- why it may be changing;
- what competing explanations remain;
- what futures are plausible;
- what evidence would distinguish them;
- what decision is justified now;
- how much authority that decision should receive.

LEWM is therefore context for governed economic intelligence, not a second
economic truth system.
