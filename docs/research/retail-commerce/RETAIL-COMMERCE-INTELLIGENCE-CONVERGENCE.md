## Mission
Preserve and develop the convergence of Retail as a Service, Retail Media, first-party shopper data, closed-loop attribution, in-store digitalization, GS1/QR/Digital Link, shelf intelligence, AI personalization, agentic commerce and FLOOOW economic truth.

FLOOOW should not copy an ad network, smart-cart company, electronic shelf-label platform or generic retailer stack. The target position is the governed intelligence layer connecting:

PRODUCT -> SUPPLIER -> IMPORT -> LANDED COST -> RETAILER -> STORE -> SHELF -> MEDIA -> SHOPPER -> PURCHASE -> REPEAT -> CONTRIBUTION -> CAPITAL -> OUTCOME.

## ABRAS’26 trigger
The ABRAS’26 material institutionalizes the Brazilian Retail as a Service direction: the retailer evolves into an orchestrator of supply, consumption, services, data, relationship and convenience. The agenda also highlights retail media as a profitability frontier, IA + QR Code, data monetization and international success cases.

This is industry-direction evidence, not implementation proof.

- Instacart: retail media, beverage use cases, smart carts, aisle-aware advertising, shelf intelligence.
- Vivino: wine label scanning, personalization and AI sommelier.

- GitHub shelf-intelligence projects: YOLO, OCR, visual embeddings, FAISS, planogram and SKU recognition.
