## Competitive reverse-engineering decision

The shared problem is early product discovery from fragmented signals. Most reviewed surfaces emphasize platform analytics, ad discovery or trade documents. **Inference from the reviewed sources:** none establishes the full chain China domestic signal → independently corroborated cross-market product identity → Brazil gap → validated factory/importability → landed economics → actual reconciled marketplace contribution. This is an unproven FLOOOW superiority hypothesis, not a claim that no competitor has it. Test coverage, identity error, false-positive opportunities, economics calibration and decision regret before building.

For each provider, a procurement-grade follow-up must check country/category coverage, historical retention, data origin, API/export, rate and license terms, model estimates versus first-party records, and whether data may be retained, combined or shown to end users. Recent changes observed during this review include EchoTik's published MCP/CLI and sales-channel analysis in its [upgrade log](https://help.echotik.live/en/articles/6870452-echotik-upgrade-log); precise release dates and entitlement require direct vendor confirmation. No integration or credential access occurred.
