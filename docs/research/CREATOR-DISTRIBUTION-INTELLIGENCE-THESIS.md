## Distribution readiness

Before substantial production capital, an opportunity should demonstrate a
plausible measurable path to customers. Future evidence might include marketplace
and search demand, organic communities, existing customer/email audience overlap,
creator/influencer/media/channel universes, retail/distributor possibilities,
paid-media and affiliate economics, community density and content velocity.

| Concept | Meaning | Insufficient substitute |
|---|---|---|
| POTENTIAL REACH | A scoped possible audience, with source limitations | A follower total presented as delivered distribution |
| QUALIFIED REACH | Category/geography/language/relevance-qualified audience | Generic popularity or engagement alone |
| ECONOMICALLY ADDRESSABLE AUDIENCE | Audience reachable under feasible costs/offers/channel constraints | Relevant people with no credible economical access path |
| OBSERVED CONVERSION | Measured actions/sales in a defined campaign/cohort/window | Intent, estimated reach or simulated sales |

Followers never equal distribution. A qualified audience still does not imply
purchase, fulfilled order or profitable customer acquisition.

## Preserve creator signals without follower-first ranking

| Family | Candidate signals |
|---|---|
| Audience size | total followers; platform-specific followers |
| Content performance | recent average views; median views; view consistency; video watch behavior where available |
| Engagement | engagement; comment depth; question frequency; comment purchase-intent signals |
| Production | content frequency; category relevance; product relevance; brand relevance |
| Audience fit | geography; language; demographics where legitimately available |
| Commercial exposure | historical sponsorship density; commercial saturation; affiliate behavior |
| Topic evidence | problem mentions; product mentions; competitor mentions; negative-product mentions |
| Trust | creator authenticity; bot/fake-audience risk |
| Economics | estimated CPM; estimated CPA; estimated activation cost; historical observed conversion when available |

Each signal needs source, period, denominator, evidence type and uncertainty.
Platform-claimed, creator-claimed, independently observed and inferred metrics
must be separately typed. A public claim is retrievable evidence of the claim,
not independent verification of its underlying value. Missing conversion is unknown.
Estimates of CPM/CPA must disclose assumptions and cannot become actual cost records.

## Audience overlap and unique reach

The supplied example of 1M + 800k + 700k followers is **not** evidence of 2.5M
distinct reachable people. Repeated followers, accounts, platforms and viewing
windows invalidate that inference. Preserve future estimation of raw reach,
duplicated reach, unique reach, category-qualified reach, geographically qualified
reach and commercially reachable audience, each with uncertainty and coverage.

For comparable sets with defensible identity evidence, unique reach is their union,
not their summed cardinality. When overlap or cross-platform identity is unavailable,
report the gap and bounded scenarios; never infer zero overlap or match people
by fuzzy names. Inferred unique reach is ESTIMATED, not OBSERVED. Population,
account/person distinctions, time windows and sampling bias must remain explicit.

## Content market intelligence

For the illustrative technical motocross boots category, future observations might
count relevant creators/videos/posts, recent content velocity, total and median
views, comments and questions; retrieve problem/brand mentions, price sensitivity,
durability/fit/availability complaints, import-brand complaints and requests for
alternatives. Language, geography, time and retrieval coverage must be recorded.

| Candidate evidence type | Interpretation to test | Boundary |
|---|---|---|
| CONTENT_DEMAND_SIGNAL | Category receives relevant content attention | Attention is not purchases or population-wide demand |
| PRODUCT_PROBLEM_SIGNAL | Retrieved complaints describe a potentially unmet need | Complaint selection/frequency is not representative defect prevalence |
| BRAND_GAP_SIGNAL | Repeated evidence suggests an unserved positioning hypothesis | A gap hypothesis is not authority to create a brand |
| DISTRIBUTION_SIGNAL | Relevant channels/communities offer a testable route | Plausible access is not delivered reach or profitable sales |

These are retrieved/inferred evidence, never canonical market truth. Keep literal
retrieval separate from interpretation, deduplicate content where justified and
retain counterexamples, sponsorship/selection effects and missing coverage.

## Creator-product fit must be decomposed

Illustrative creator: motocross specialist, Brazilian riders, equipment-test
content, historical boots/protection/helmets coverage, low commercial saturation
and possible Redmoto audience overlap. “HIGH fit” is a hypothetical conclusion,
not an actual ranked creator or a single opaque score.

Expose category relevance, audience overlap, geography, commercial saturation,
content performance, product authority, trust indicators, cost and historical
actionability. Each component carries evidence, uncertainty and dependencies.
Any aggregation must expose assumptions and sensitivity; follower count cannot
silently dominate or compensate for missing economic or product-fit evidence.

## Creator portfolio versus single influencer

Research a portfolio of micro creators, mid-tier category authorities, possibly
one or two larger creators, owned channels and paid retargeting. This is a candidate
experiment, not a chosen campaign. Compare cost, unique reach, category precision,
conversion, concentration risk, content durability and organic reuse potential.
Reuse rights and audience/channel dependency need independent qualification.

The research question is which portfolio produces the best expected economic
distribution for this opportunity, subject to overlap, uncertainty, cash and loss
limits. Include alternative portfolios and absence of economically viable options.
Do not optimize for celebrity or deduplicated reach without contribution economics.

## Pre-launch proof and capital linkage

Preserve discovery → product prototype → creator validation → audience validation
→ small market test → scale. The companion thesis maps this to G0–G6 and requires
evidence, capital, maximum loss, success and kill rules at each transition.

Future tests could validate creator interest, selected sample response, content,
waitlists/intent, landing pages and a small batch. Reservations/deposits require
separate legal/commercial qualification. Each funnel step is a different observation;
none substitutes for fulfilled profitable sales. Predetermine exposure and success
criteria, preserve negative outcomes, and compare against credible alternatives.

## Typed predictability

| Statement | Required type and scope |
|---|---|
| RAW_FOLLOWER_REACH | OBSERVED_FROM_PUBLIC_PROFILES means displayed counts, not verified unique people |
| UNIQUE_REACH | ESTIMATED; disclose overlap model and uncertainty |
| EXPECTED_VIDEO_VIEWS | SIMULATED; disclose assumptions/calibration |
| CREATOR_POST_COMMITMENT | CONTRACTED only with actual supporting agreement; not observed delivery |
| ACTUAL_VIDEO_VIEWS | OBSERVED/ACTUAL for defined content, source and as-of window |
| ACTUAL_SALES | OBSERVED/ACTUAL after canonical economic reconciliation |

OBSERVED, INFERRED, ESTIMATED, SIMULATED, CONTRACTED and ACTUAL must remain distinct.
Do not state “this launch will reach3 million people” as a prediction of certainty.
Even a contractual commitment retains its exact deliverable scope; delivery
requires separate observation, and neither proves conversion or contribution.

## Distribution learning loop

Creator → content → views → clicks → conversion → CAC → returns → contribution
→ repeat purchase. Preserve campaign/cohort/windows, source lineage, attribution
assumptions and reconciliation to actual costs/orders/refunds. A correlation or
last-click attribution is not a causal claim about incrementality.

Future learning should identify selling creators, converting audiences, effective
categories, failed forecasts and resilient distribution, and connect to supplier,
brand-extension and capital-gate outcomes. Track calibration and downside before
proposing more autonomy. No unobserved success or fabricated completeness enters
canonical truth. The next research step is a falsifiable test design and evidence
availability assessment; actual tests remain separately authorized.
