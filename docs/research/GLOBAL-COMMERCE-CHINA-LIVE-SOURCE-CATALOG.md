## Event granularity and provenance

A research sample should link, where available and permitted: platform; market and audience geography; live-room ID; session start/end; creator/account ID; product/item and variant ID; product-introduction interval; observed viewer/engagement events; offer price and currency; stock display; attributed order/units/GMV metric with **first-party, vendor-estimated or self-reported** basis; collection/source timestamps; API/license; and replay/document reference. Missing IDs or replay mean `UNAVAILABLE`, not an inferred join. A chart without a known denominator or metric definition cannot quantify conversion.

Separate factory livestreams, MCNs, brands and independent creators by **verified role evidence**, not account title. A product recurring across hosts may be syndicated content or affiliate propagation from one source, not independent corroboration. Identity joins across Douyin/Taobao/Kuaishou/Xiaohongshu/TikTok and Brazil require the [blind identity benchmark](GLOBAL-COMMERCE-PRODUCT-IDENTITY-BENCHMARK.md). Chronological diffusion claims require observation-time snapshots and negative controls; an archive discovered later must not be backdated to when FLOOOW would have known it.

## Access decision

First test merchant-authorized official paths for the merchant's own outcomes. Then trial licensed analytics providers with disclosed estimation methods and explicit retention/combination/display rights. Review Chinese and international market entitlement separately. Reject unauthorized scraping or a synthetic “global GMV” assembled from incomparable metrics. No provider access, live API call or implementation occurred.
