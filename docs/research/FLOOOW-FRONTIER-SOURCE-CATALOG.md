# FLOOOW Frontier Source Catalog

> Status: RESEARCH EVIDENCE
>
> External sources provide market evidence and design context only.
> They never become FLOOOW truth authority.

## Evidence Classification

- ADOPT: capability or principle is directly useful.
- ADAPT: concept is useful only after applying FLOOOW governance constraints.
- REJECT: direction conflicts with FLOOOW invariants or category strategy.
- WATCH: material signal, no current design decision.

## 1. BlackLine

### Signal

BlackLine publicly positions its platform as Agentic Financial Operations and describes a control/trust layer for CFO adoption of AI.

Verity Prepare is described as a multi-agent system for governed reconciliation with transparency, auditability and human oversight.

### Sources

- https://investors.blackline.com/news-releases/news-release-details/blackline-unveils-agentic-financial-operations-close-ais
- https://investors.blackline.com/news-releases/news-release-details/blackline-advances-governed-ai-finance-general-availability

### FLOOOW Classification

ADAPT

### Implication

Governed agentic finance is becoming an enterprise category, so generic claims such as auditability, immutable trails and human oversight will not remain differentiators by themselves.

FLOOOW must differentiate at the level of evidence-bound economic truth, explicit authority and action eligibility.

## 2. Rillet

Its strategy combines AI agents, a real-time financial system of record, human approval and audit trail.

### Sources

- https://www.rillet.com/blog/100m-series-c

### FLOOOW Classification

ADAPT

### Implication

Do not compete by becoming another AI-native ERP.

Use the signal to validate demand for trustworthy agentic finance while occupying a distinct economic-trust/control-plane layer.

## 3. NIST NCCoE

### Signal

NIST published a 2026 concept paper focused on software and AI agent identity and authorization, explicitly calling out identification, authorization, auditing and non-repudiation.

### Source

- https://csrc.nist.gov/pubs/other/2026/02/05/accelerating-the-adoption-of-software-and-ai-agent/ipd

### FLOOOW Classification

ADOPT

### Implication

Agent identity and authorization should be treated as first-class external interoperability concerns.

They remain distinct from economic authority.

## 4. Google Agent Payments Protocol / AP2

### Signal

Google donated AP2 to the FIDO Alliance in 2026 and introduced support for Human Not Present autonomous payments.

AP2-compatible Verifiable Intent is intended to provide a tamper-resistant record of user-authorized agent actions.

### Source

- https://blog.google/products-and-platforms/platforms/google-pay/agent-payments-protocol-fido-alliance/

### FLOOOW Classification

ADAPT

### Implication

AP2 can help prove authorization to transact.

FLOOOW's opportunity is complementary: prove whether the economic premise behind the transaction is sufficiently governed and evidenced.

## 5. OpenAI Agentic Commerce Protocol / ACP

### Signal

ACP is an open standard for agentic commerce connecting agents, people and merchants while keeping merchants in control of payment and fulfillment systems.

OpenAI expanded ACP in 2026 to support richer product discovery.

### Sources

- https://openai.com/index/buy-it-in-chatgpt/
- https://openai.com/index/powering-product-discovery-in-chatgpt/

### FLOOOW Classification

WATCH / ADAPT

### Implication

Agentic commerce standards are moving up the transaction stack.

FLOOOW should study compatibility while avoiding dependence on a single commerce protocol.

## 8. Evidence Governance

External claims must be stored with:

- source URL;
- publication date when known;
- observation date;
- source type;
- claim summary;
- confidence in interpretation;
- ADOPT / ADAPT / REJECT / WATCH classification.

External evidence may influence research priorities.

It may never grant economic authority inside FLOOOW.
