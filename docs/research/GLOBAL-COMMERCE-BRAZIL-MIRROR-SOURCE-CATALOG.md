## Measurement contract for a later controlled sample

Capture source URL/endpoint, license/entitlement, query, market, category, collection and source timestamps, listing/product/variation/seller IDs, price/currency/shipping, brand, stock display, ad marker, review count/rating and question count only where actually exposed. Store field-level `OBSERVED`, `DERIVED`, `UNAVAILABLE`, `CONTRADICTED`; do not fill missing values with zero. Retain snapshot-to-snapshot history **only if permitted**. Derive seller count, brand concentration, price quantiles, listing/review growth, stock persistence and ad density with the queried population, deduplication rule, observation window and uncertainty attached. Platform ranking or review velocity cannot substitute for independent unit sales.

The mirror should require a blind product-identity judgment before cross-platform aggregation. Preserve candidate and conflict edges, packaging/variant/quantity differences and source origin; do not merge by image or title alone. Assess gap hypotheses against independent Brazil demand, actual listing coverage, authorized performance and regulatory/importability screens. Sampling bias, search personalization, unavailable history and geographic access are explicit blockers to a saturation or open-window conclusion.

No API credentials were used, no collector was built and no marketplace-wide completeness claim is made.
