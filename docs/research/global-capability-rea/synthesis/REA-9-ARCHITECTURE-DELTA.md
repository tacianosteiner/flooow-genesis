## Candidate moat hypothesis

FLOOOW's potential moat is not superior access to data, AI, agents,
automation or analytics.

The candidate moat is a governed economic epistemology encoded into the
operating system:

evidence cannot silently become truth;

estimates cannot silently become observations;

partial values cannot silently become totals;

contradictions cannot silently disappear;

authority to act cannot silently become authority to claim;

predicted value cannot silently become realized value.
