## Strongest surviving invariant

Information must never silently acquire stronger epistemic status.

Therefore:

UNKNOWN != ZERO

UNQUANTIFIED != ZERO

PARTIAL != COMPLETE

ESTIMATED != OBSERVED

PREDICTED != ACTUAL

CONTRADICTED != RESOLVED

AUTHORIZED_TO_CALCULATE != AUTHORIZED_TO_CLAIM

AUTHORIZED_TO_CLAIM != PROVEN_TRUE

AUTHORIZED_TO_CLAIM != AUTHORIZED_TO_PUBLISH

AUTHORIZED_TO_ACT != AUTHORIZED_TO_CLAIM

## Strategic conclusion

FLOOOW should not attempt to beat every benchmark at its own specialty.

Palantir-class world modeling can become evidence/context infrastructure.

ServiceNow-class runtime governance can become execution authority
infrastructure.

Celonis-class process intelligence can become process evidence.

Pacvue/CommerceIQ-class commerce intelligence can become commerce
evidence and execution infrastructure.

Salesforce-class enterprise context can become context and provenance
infrastructure.

UiPath-class orchestration can become durable execution infrastructure.

FLOOOW's candidate ownership layer sits orthogonally:

what economic assertion is justified,
what remains unknown,
what remains contradicted,
what may be published,
and what economic effect was actually proven after action.
