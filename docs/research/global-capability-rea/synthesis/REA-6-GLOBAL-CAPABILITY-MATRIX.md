## Interpretation

FLOOOW is currently behind mature enterprise platforms in most
production capabilities.

This is not evidence against the architecture.

It determines where FLOOOW should consume, adapt or interoperate rather
than attempt to recreate established infrastructure.

The candidate differentiation is not a single feature.

It is the composition of:

evidence
→ admissibility
→ calculation
→ claim authority
→ proof-bearing claim
→ publication authority
→ decision
→ action
→ actual outcome
→ canonical reconciliation
→ realized economic truth
→ belief update

No integrated public equivalent to that complete economic semantic
chain was established during REA-1 through REA-5.

This means:

NOT_ESTABLISHED_FROM_PUBLIC_EVIDENCE.

It does not mean:

COMPETITORS_DO_NOT_HAVE_IT.
