Candidate definition:

> completeness or authority than the admissible economic evidence
> supporting it.

Required distinctions:

- PARTIAL != COMPLETE
- UNKNOWN != ZERO
- UNQUANTIFIED != ZERO
- CONTRADICTED != RESOLVED
- ESTIMATED != OBSERVED
- PREDICTED != ACTUAL
- AUTHORIZED_TO_ACT != AUTHORIZED_TO_CLAIM

## Claim Authority vs Action Authority

FLOOOW candidate separation:

ACTION_AUTHORITY != CLAIM_AUTHORITY

ACTION_AUTHORITY:

Authority to execute a governed action.

CLAIM_AUTHORITY:

Authority to assert a bounded economic claim under a defined truth and
evidence context.

Neither automatically implies truth.

CLAIM_AUTHORITY != PROVEN_TRUE

Example:

An agent may be authorized to pause Ads.

That does not imply that the same agent may publish:

"This SKU loses R$17.42 per order"

as canonical Economic Truth.

## Governed Economic Publication

A component may be validly calculated without the aggregate being authorized
for publication as a complete economic total.

Example:

Ads leakage = 40k  
Returns leakage = 25k  
Freight leakage = UNQUANTIFIED

Allowed:

KNOWN_QUANTIFIED_COMPONENTS=65k

Not automatically allowed:

TOTAL_ECONOMIC_LEAKAGE=65k

Existing FLOOOW invariant:

ANY REQUIRED STAGE = UNQUANTIFIED
→ totalQuantifiedLeakage = null

This motivates three separate authority concepts:

- CLAIM_AUTHORITY
- PUBLICATION_AUTHORITY
- ACTION_AUTHORITY

They must not be collapsed.
