## Candidate FLOOOW difference

The surviving question is not whether Pacvue or CommerceIQ can compute
economics.

They can.

The surviving question is:

Under what evidence and authority may a computed economic statement be
promoted into a canonical economic claim?

RAW SIGNAL
→ EVIDENCE
→ ADMISSIBILITY

→ PROOF-BEARING ECONOMIC CLAIM
