Marketplace operations provide:

- dense transaction data;

governed Economic Truth.

## Autonomous economic agents

An autonomous economic agent eventually needs two independent answers.

### CAN I DO IT?

Authorization.

### SHOULD I DO IT?

Economic Truth.

A governed autonomous action requires both.

Authorization must not substitute for truth.

Evidence must not substitute for authority.

## New north-star question

Can an economic truth governed inside FLOOOW become a verifiable proof
outside FLOOOW?

Today FLOOOW is primarily designed around truth inside an organizational
boundary.

A future system may need another company, bank, insurer, marketplace,
auditor or autonomous agent to verify a bounded economic claim without
receiving:

- the issuer database;
- provider credentials;
- all internal evidence;
- the complete private decision graph.

## WITHDRAWN semantics

WITHDRAWN must not be described as arbitration between competing truths.

Its intended meaning is:

CONFIRMED(O1,M1)
→ WITHDRAWN(O1,M1)

WITHDRAWN revokes the current authority of the previously confirmed
relation.

It does not:

- prove M1 false;
- confirm M2;
- transfer authority;
- silently remap identity;
- resolve a cross-organization dispute.

## Architecture compatibility rule

Even while external attestations remain research-only, new foundational
architecture should avoid making them impossible.

Preserve:

1. deterministic semantic fingerprints;
2. Evidence / Identity / Claim / Authority separation;
3. immutable lineage;
4. semantic versioning;
5. explicit supersession;
6. explicit withdrawal;
7. tenant isolation;
8. fail-closed behavior;
9. no silent correction;
10. no authority inferred from evidence.

This is a compatibility guardrail.

It is not a feature request.
